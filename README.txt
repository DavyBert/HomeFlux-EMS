HomeFlux EMS — Your energy, arranged differently

HomeFlux EMS is a hardware-independent energy management system for Homey Pro. It coordinates home batteries, solar production, up to four EV chargers, up to four HVAC devices and an optional water heater around your energy contract, solar forecast and grid limits, with Peak Guard keeping priority.

Integrations use bilingual Homey Flow cards, so HomeFlux can work with different brands. Flexible thermal loads are started from a configurable low-frequency priority list, while EVs retain their own PV, tariff and charging-session logic.
